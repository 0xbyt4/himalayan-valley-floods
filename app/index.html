    26 August 2026 · Rasuwa to Chitwan, Nepal

    The Bhote Koshi – Trishuli flood

    At 08:37 rock and ice broke away above the Lhende Glacier. The flood ran 193 km down the Bhote Koshi and Trishuli rivers and reached Devghat in the afternoon.

    ⓘNot a warning system. This page rebuilds that day from public reports and a flood model. The water is shown with its uncertainty.
